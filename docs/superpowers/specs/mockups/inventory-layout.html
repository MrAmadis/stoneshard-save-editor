How should the inventory look?

Both use your real save (Velmir, exitsave_1) and the real game icons. Click an item to edit it in the panel on the right. Click A or B to pick one.

A

Like the game: equipment slots + the real bag grid

Items keep their real size and position (torch is 1×3, brigandine 2×3). You see free space at a glance and could drag items around. Added items drop into the first gap that fits.
